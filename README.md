## Welcome

Keck.ai is a home for ideas, tools, and collaborations aimed at creating useful, responsible solutions for humanity. We’re here to learn in the open, work with care, and turn promising ideas into practical impact.

## What guides us

- **People first:** Start with real needs and keep human wellbeing at the center.
- **Build responsibly:** Consider safety, privacy, accessibility, and the wider effects of our work.
- **Stay curious:** Share what we learn, welcome thoughtful feedback, and improve together.
- **Make it useful:** Favor clear, practical solutions over technology for its own sake.

## What we're building

This GitHub profile is the starting point. As projects take shape, we’ll share their goals, documentation, and ways to get involved here.

## Build with us

Have an idea, question, or perspective to share? We welcome collaborators, community feedback, and constructive conversations. Open an issue in a project repository when one is available, or start a discussion with us through GitHub.
